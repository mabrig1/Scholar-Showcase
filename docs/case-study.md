# Engineering case study: Scholar

## Problem
Researchers need traceable sources, accessible metadata, transparent evidence assessment and responsible publication workflows.

## Design approach
Separate discovery from verification, record provenance, distinguish support from contradiction, require human assessment of evidence and check sharing rights before public deposits. Preserve stable bibliographic landing pages and machine-readable metadata.

## Portfolio evidence
The independent example models relationships between fictional claims and evidence records. It does not retrieve articles, validate real DOI registrations, evaluate scientific truth or perform live AI inference. The original project's documented functionality includes research workflows and moderated publication; deployment and indexing outcomes are not independently verified here.

## Interview discussion
Research data modelling, provenance, metadata interoperability, access controls, evidence uncertainty, reproducibility and scholarly integrity.
